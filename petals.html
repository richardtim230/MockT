Founder's Vision

Petals of Hope Foundation

"We are driven by a simple yet profound mandate: to be God's hand extended to a world in need, bringing light, healing broken hearts, and fanning the flame of purpose in every young life."

Every child deserves to know that their current story is not their final destination. Through our school outreaches, academic competitions, and community drives, we witness lives transformed, faith renewed, and hope restored.

Our first outreach to Acada High School, Modakeke on January 23rd, 2026, where over 40 young souls gave their hearts to God, stands as a testament to what happens when love is put into action.
