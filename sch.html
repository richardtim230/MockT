✅ School Results Verification System

One of our flagship offerings is the School Results Verification System. This secure gateway enables academic institutions, higher education facilities, and corporate organizations to seamlessly verify the authenticity of student credentials.

By securely inputting student details and using an institutional scratch card, evaluators can instantly confirm that the grades and academic performances displayed were originally issued by your accredited institution. This adds credibility and prevents fraud.

How it benefits your school:

  • ✓ ExamGuard provides verification scratch cards to your school for FREE
  • ✓ Your school can resell these cards to students at any price you choose
  • ✓ Creates an independent revenue stream for your institution
  • ✓ Students get authentic credential verification
  • ✓ Employers and universities trust verified results

🤝 A Mutually Beneficial Model

To support school operations, ExamGuard provides these verification scratch cards to institutions entirely free of charge. Schools then retain the flexibility to resell these cards to students at a rate they deem appropriate, creating a sustainable and independent revenue stream for the institution.
